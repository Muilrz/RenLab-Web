What we look for

Our work is interdisciplinary: multimodal Raman and electrochemical platforms (SERS, TERS, dark-field spectroscopy, nanoelectrochemistry), home-built instrumentation, and AI methods for spectral denoising, quantitative analysis and spectrum–structure correlation — applied to batteries, electrochemical interfaces and biological systems. Backgrounds in chemistry, physics, optics, electronics, materials, biology, computer science or software engineering are all welcome.

Interested candidates are encouraged to email a CV and a short statement of research interests to any of the contacts on this page.
